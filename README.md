## ⚙️ How It Works

The program takes periodic, low-resolution snapshots of your current screen. It sends these images to a vision model. This model identifies buttons, text boxes, and icons on your screen. It then decides which mouse clicks or keyboard inputs will help you reach your current goal. 

You remain in control at all times. You can pause, resume, or quit the process whenever you want. The software acts as a partner that suggests actions for you to approve.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

We prioritize your privacy. The system captures screenshots only when you activate the assistant feature. The process transmits these images over secure connections. No private files stay on our servers. You can clear your history and logs at any time through the Settings menu.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you run into problems, check these common fixes first.

* The app does not open: Ensure that you have a stable internet connection. Check if your antivirus software blocked the installation.
* The assistant is slow: Close other programs that consume high amounts of memory. Your vision model needs bandwidth to process your screen images.
* The screen is not captured: Verify that the app has permission to access your desktop within your Windows Privacy settings.
* Errors appear during tasks: Click the Refresh button in the main dashboard. This resets the visual connection between your computer and the vision service.

## 📈 Tips for Best Results

Use these practices to improve how the software helps you.

* Keep your active window clear. Do not overlap too many windows if you want the app to see buttons clearly.
* Use clean desktop backgrounds. High-contrast images help the model recognize your workspace components.
* Define clear tasks. The software performs best when you have a specific goal in mind, such as filling out a web form or organizing files.
* Monitor activity. Watch the small feedback window to see what the assistant plans to do next.

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this software record my entire day?**
No. It only activates when you trigger a specific task or request. You decide when the vision mode is active.

**Can I stop it instantly?**
Yes. You can press the Escape key at any time to halt all mouse and keyboard actions from the software.

**Does it save my passwords?**
No. The vision model identifies user interface elements but does not store or read personal credentials. It simulates your inputs based on visual layout.

**Do I need a paid plan?**
The software requires a connection to a vision service. You should check the current pricing models of the provider to see if their free tiers meet your daily usage needs.

**Can I run it on multiple monitors?**
The software focuses on the primary display by default. You can adjust this in the display settings inside the app.
